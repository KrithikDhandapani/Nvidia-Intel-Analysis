#  Intel Investment Impact Analysis

This project investigates the **financial and market impact of NVIDIA’s investment in Intel (September 2025)** using **Python** for data processing, event study analysis, and anomaly detection, and **Tableau** for visualization and storytelling.

The goal was to perform a **real-world event study** — quantifying how Intel’s stock and its competitors reacted before and after the announcement — and to translate the findings into **data-driven insights for investors and industry analysts**.

---

##  Project Overview

As a data analyst, my objective was to assess **how a major industry event (NVIDIA investing in Intel)** influenced Intel’s market performance and investor sentiment.

The dataset includes **daily stock prices for Intel (INTC)** and its semiconductor peers — **AMD, TSM, and Qualcomm** — over the surrounding event window.

Using **Python**, I performed:
- Stock return calculations  
- Industry average and abnormal return computations  
- Cumulative Abnormal Return (CAR) estimation  
- Exported a clean dataset for Tableau visualization  

---

##  Core Questions Answered

1. How did Intel’s stock price and volume respond immediately after NVIDIA’s investment announcement?  
2. Did Intel outperform or underperform relative to its semiconductor peers post-announcement?  
3. What were the **abnormal returns** and **cumulative abnormal returns (CAR)** surrounding the event?  
4. What can we project about Intel’s short-term trajectory after the investment?  

---

##  Key Insights

###  Event Impact
- Intel’s stock experienced a **sharp positive abnormal return on the day of NVIDIA’s investment**, suggesting strong investor optimism.  
- Competitor stocks (AMD, TSM, QCOM) showed **mild positive spillover**, reflecting broader confidence in the semiconductor sector.

###  Industry Comparison
- Pre-investment, Intel’s returns largely mirrored the industry average.  
- Post-investment, Intel outperformed its peers — indicating a **market-perceived competitive boost**.

###  Cumulative Abnormal Returns (CAR)
- CAR analysis showed Intel’s **largest sustained gains** occurred within the first few trading days following the announcement, before stabilizing.  

###  Projection
- Short-term projections suggest a **moderate upward trend** in Intel’s valuation, contingent on execution of collaborative synergies with NVIDIA.
